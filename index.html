About this estimate. This calculator is based on a statistical model and is intended for general educational purposes only. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Individual risk can be affected by factors not captured here. Please discuss your results with a qualified healthcare provider.

All medical decisions should be made by the patient in consultation with her doctor. The authors and sponsors of this tool accept no responsibility for any clinical use or misuse of the risk score it produces.

This algorithm was developed by academic researchers at Tel Aviv University, based on routinely collected health data from many thousands of women who are members of Maccabi Healthcare Services in Israel.
